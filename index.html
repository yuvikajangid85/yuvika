Flagship Innovation

"ThriftLoop" – The Campus-Only Resale & Swap App

A closed-loop marketplace empowering university students to buy, sell, swap, and rent semester essentials safely within their own college.

The Campus Problem

  • Hefty Semester Expenses: Freshers spend ₹3,000–₹6,000 every semester on items needed for barely 120 days.
  • Massive Material Waste: Unsold books, lab coats, and drafters rot in hostel lockers or get dumped in trash.
  • Public Platform Risks: OLX and Facebook Marketplace attract strangers, require expensive couriers, and expose students to frauds.
  • One-Time Use Burden: Interview blazers and lab gear are purchased for single events at steep price tags.

The ThriftLoop Solution

  • Hyperlocal Circularity: Only JECRC students allowed via verified college email login.
  • Zero Delivery Fees: Safe, physical meetups right at the Central Library, Canteen, or Main Lawn.
  • Smart Multi-Mode: Buy, Sell, Swap, or Rent based on specific academic timelines.
  • Green Gamification: Rewarding each circular exchange with Green Points redeemable for food and stationery.

Sustainable Business & Revenue Model

Designed for sustainable scale, campus adoption, and mutual value for students and local merchants.

Core Engine

3% – 5% Transaction Commission

A modest transaction fee on successful equipment sales and peer-to-peer rentals (blazers, drafters, calculators) processed with buyer protection escrow.

Keeps transactions 90% cheaper than courier or marketplace alternatives.
Local Commerce

Sponsored Listings & Local Vendors

Local Sitapura & Jaipur student services — PG accommodations, coaching institutes, print/photocopy shops, and laptop repair hubs — pay for verified student-facing sponsored cards.

Targeted campus demographics with zero spam.
Partnerships

Campus Cafe & Stationery Partnerships

Strategic tie-ups with campus canteens and stationery stores to redeem earned Green Points. ThriftLoop collects lead generation and partnership fee margins on redeemed coupons.

Drives repeat footfall directly to campus vendors.
