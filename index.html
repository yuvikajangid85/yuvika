⚠️

The Painful Reality Today

"Cleaning household overhead water tanks is difficult, messy, and time-consuming. People often have to manually enter or reach inside the tank, and many households postpone cleaning because of the inconvenience."

  • ❌
    Dangerous Confined Space: Workers or homeowners must squeeze into narrow, dark, low-oxygen tank manholes.
  • ❌
    Unhygienic & Labor-Intensive: Sludge, toxic biofilm, bacterial colonies, and algae accumulate because tanks are cleaned only once a year or less.
  • ❌
    Massive Water Wastage: Traditional manual cleaning drains thousands of liters of usable water down the drain just to sweep.
  • ❌
    Postponed Health Risk: Waterborne diseases spread silently when families delay cleaning due to high labor costs and hassle.
💡

Shweta's Robotic Innovation

"A compact, remotely operated robotic device designed to navigate inside overhead water tanks and mechanically remove sludge, sediment, algae, and biofilm using rotating brushes or scrubbing mechanisms."

  • ✅
    Zero Human Entry: Slips easily through standard tank lids (300mm–450mm). Safe, hands-off operation from the outside!
  • ✅
    Mechanical Dual Rotating Scrubbers: High-torque nylon & microfiber brushes vigorously scour stubborn sediment and algae layers.
  • ✅
    Integrated Sludge & Slurry Extraction: Integrated submersible pump vacuums dislodged sediment directly out via exhaust pipe.
  • ✅
    Wireless Telemetry & Control: Controlled remotely via smartphone app or handheld transmitter with live inspection camera feedback.

🛠️ How the Robotic System Works

Engineered for affordability, rugged submersible performance, and effortless deployment.

🔄

Rotating Scrubbing System

Counter-rotating bristles dislodge thick algae, calcium scales, and biofilm from both tank floors and curved vertical walls without scratching plastic or cement.

🌪️

Slurry Extraction Mechanism

Equipped with a high-flow diaphragm slurry pump that vacuums loosened debris directly out through an external discharge hose without needing to empty the entire tank.

📡

Wireless Remote Guidance

Wi-Fi / Bluetooth telemetry allows the operator to steer the bot, adjust scrubbing speeds, and inspect hard-to-see corners safely from the roof terrace.

🛡️

IP68 Submersible Hull

Compact, waterproof aluminum-composite chassis sealed with food-grade silicone, ensuring no rust, no oil leaks, and zero chemical contamination.

🌟 My Journey & Philosophy

As a 1st year B.Tech student at JECRC University, I believe the greatest engineering projects don't come from sitting in quiet lecture halls—they come from observing the uncomfortable, messy problems people deal with every single week.

Watching people dread overhead water tank cleaning or climb into cramped, moldy spaces made me realize: why should a human ever have to risk their health for a task a robotic crawler can do ten times faster?

I chose a Shinchan theme because Shinchan represents fearlessness, relentless curiosity, thinking completely out of the box, and never taking obstacles too seriously. That's the exact energy I bring to prototyping and startup innovation!
